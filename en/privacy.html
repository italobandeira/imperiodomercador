Privacy Policy

Empire of the Merchant was built to work without an account or a server. This policy explains what stays on your device, what the ad and payment services may receive, and the choices you have.

Effective October 21, 2026

Your progress stays on your device and, with Play Games, in your Google account. Nothing is sent to iFlag.
Rewarded videos are optional, come from Unity LevelPlay and are not personalized.
Purchases go through Google Play. iFlag never sees your payment details.

In short

You don't need an account. Your game progress is saved on your device and, if you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy is kept in your Google account so you can restore it on another phone. The game uses no analytics tools and builds no profiles. Rewarded videos, always optional, are delivered by Unity LevelPlay, which processes technical data and the advertising ID to show non-personalized ads, measure delivery and prevent fraud. Purchases are processed by Google Play.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the Empire of the Merchant game for Android and to related support communications.

The game is offered by Italo Bandeira, under the iFlag brand, based in Brazil. For the data processing under our responsibility, we act as the controller under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, LGPD) and other applicable laws.

Contact and data rights requests: iflagdev@gmail.com.

3. How and why we use data

We only use information to:

  • save your progress and calculate what happens while you're away, such as offline earnings;
  • keep a copy of your progress in your Google Play Games account and offer it on a new device;
  • schedule the local notifications you allowed;
  • show the rewarded videos you choose to watch, deliver the bonus, measure delivery and fight fraud;
  • deliver and confirm purchases made through Google Play and restore permanent purchases;
  • answer support, privacy and data rights requests;
  • comply with legal obligations and protect rights when necessary.

Legal bases may include performance of the Terms of Use, consent where required (for example, for notifications), legitimate interests that respect your rights, compliance with legal obligations and the exercise of rights.

We do not sell data, we do not build behavioral profiles and we do not use your data to personalize ads.

4. Ads, purchases and other services

Rewarded videos (Unity LevelPlay)

Videos only appear when you tap a video button to earn a bonus. They are delivered by Unity LevelPlay, from Unity Technologies, which may use the advertising ID, IP address and device signals to deliver the ad, cap repetitions, produce reports and prevent fraud.

Before starting the service, the game signals that there is no consent for personalized advertising and asks that data not be sold. As a result, the ads shown are not personalized. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the ad service is not started.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google > Ads). Learn more in Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Cloud progress (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games (sign-in is usually automatic on Android), the game uploads a copy of your progress to your account from time to time and when you leave the game. When you open the game on a device with less progress, it shows both and asks which one to use. iFlag has no access to this copy: it stays with Google's services, under the Google Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Play Games or delete game data in the Play Games app settings.

In-game purchases (Google Play)

Purchases use Google Play's payment system. The game checks with Google Play to confirm payment and restore permanent purchases. iFlag does not receive or store card numbers, billing addresses or other payment data. That processing follows the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Android automatic backup

If Google backup is on, Android may include the game data in your Google Account to restore it on a new device. This backup is made by the system, without access by iFlag, and can be turned off in Android settings.

International transfers

Unity, Google and their providers may process data in other countries. In those cases, processing follows the safeguards described by each provider and the applicable data protection rules.

5. How long data is kept

  • Game progress: stays on the device until you use "Erase progress" in the Options, clear the app data or uninstall the game.
  • Cloud copy: stays in your Play Games account until you delete the game data in the Play Games app or your Google Account; each upload replaces the previous one.
  • Notifications: are canceled when you turn them off in the Options or in Android, and replaced every time you leave the game.
  • Ad and purchase data: kept by Unity and Google according to their own policies, settings and legal obligations.
  • Support messages: for as long as needed to resolve the request and, where applicable, to meet obligations or protect rights.

Because progress lives on the device and in your Google account, iFlag cannot recover or delete it remotely.

6. Your choices and rights

Under the LGPD and other applicable laws (such as US state privacy laws), you may request confirmation and access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion, portability, information about sharing, objection, withdrawal of consent and information about the consequences of not consenting.

You can also act directly:

  • delete your progress with the "Erase progress" button in the game Options;
  • turn off notifications in the game Options or Android settings;
  • reset or delete your advertising ID in Android;
  • skip the videos: they are always optional;
  • turn off Android automatic backup or delete the copy in your Google Account.

For data under our control, write to iflagdev@gmail.com. We may ask for minimal information to confirm your identity. Exercising your rights is free.

7. Security

We take technical and administrative measures appropriate to the risks of the service. The game keeps no server with player data. Still, no device or transmission is completely immune to incidents; we recommend keeping your screen lock and system up to date.

8. Children and teens

Empire of the Merchant is rated for ages 13 and up and is not directed to children. The game builds no profiles, shows no personalized ads and has no paid loot boxes, in line with child protection laws such as Brazil's Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents (Law No. 15,211/2025) and the US COPPA.

Purchases depend on a Google Account and can be protected with a password or Google Play parental controls (Family Link). If you believe a child's data was sent to iFlag improperly, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the game, in third-party services or in the law. The current version is on this page, with its update date. If a change requires a new choice or consent, we will let you know in the game.
