From a potion stall to an empire

You start with Pip, an apprentice behind a wooden counter. Heroes come down the street looking for potions, herbs and swords, and every sale pays for the next shop. Hire managers, send parties into the dungeon and watch the gold keep coming in, even with the game closed.

How it works

  1. 1

    Open shops

    Eight shops, from potions to relics. Every purchase speeds up production, and unit milestones double the speed.

  2. 2

    Serve the heroes

    Warriors, mages, archers and clerics line up at your counter. Tap them for bonuses and send them against the Goblin King, the Lich and the Dragon.

  3. 3

    Start over stronger

    Prestige, earn crowns and unlock the 16 nodes of the Skill Tree. Then take your trade to four more kingdoms.

What's inside

  • Managers who sell for you

    Old Bruna, Thorin the dwarf, Greta the armorer and others run your shops. Nix the goblin runs all of them at once.

  • Dungeon and bosses

    Build a party of four heroes, climb floors and defeat animated bosses to earn essence and unlock new shops.

  • Five kingdoms

    Brumavale Village, Lumen Forest, Ironforge Mountain, Mirkmarsh Swamp and the Abyssal City, each with its own scenery, shops and outfit for Pip.

  • Merchant's Fair

    Every weekend, a three-day event with themed stalls, its own dungeon and prizes delivered on the spot.

  • Hero album

    Collect legendary heroes like Sir Aldric and Lyra. Every copy gives a permanent bonus that survives prestige.

  • Gold while you sleep

    Your managers keep earning while you're away. When you come back, a full chest is waiting.

Fair play

  • Nothing paid is random

    No paid loot boxes. Everything you can buy shows its contents first.

  • Videos are always optional

    Watch a video only if you want a bonus. No ad ever interrupts your game.

  • No account, no internet

    Your progress lives on your phone. Play on a plane, on the subway or in line.

  • Rated 13+

    Light fantasy, no blood and no gambling. Ads are never personalized.

Frequently asked questions

Is the game free?

Yes. There are optional purchases, like the Starter Pack and the Dungeon Pass, and optional videos that give bonuses. You can play everything without paying.

Do I need internet?

Not to play. The internet is only used to load rewarded videos and to make purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress is saved on the device itself, with no login.

I got a new phone. Will I lose my progress?

The game has no server and no login. If Google backup is on in your Android settings, the system can restore the game data on your new device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes your progress.

Are there paid loot boxes?

No. Rare heroes and managers come from direct purchases with visible contents, from progress, from events or from the pass. Nothing paid is random.

Which languages are available?

English, Portuguese and Spanish. You can switch languages in the Options.

How do I contact the developer?

Through the contact page or by email at iflagdev@gmail.com.
